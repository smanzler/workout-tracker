Rep is a simple log for your lifts. Record every set, see your personal records as you break them, and keep up with your friends' training.

LOG YOUR WORKOUTS
- Record the weight and reps of every set, with the sets from your last workout as suggestions.
- Pick from a library of exercises, or add your own.
- Save routines and start a workout from one.
- Your workout in progress stays on your phone, so closing the app loses nothing.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
- Rep marks a personal record when you beat your best weight, volume or estimated one-rep max.
- Each exercise shows your records, a chart of your heaviest weight and every session.
- Browse and edit your workout history.

TRAIN WITH FRIENDS
Follow friends, see their workouts in your feed and give kudos. Get a notification when someone follows you or gives kudos to your workout.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import your workouts from a Strong CSV export.
